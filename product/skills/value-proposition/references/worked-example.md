# Worked Example: End-to-End Value Proposition

Read this when you want to see the complete output of this skill for one segment. The product, the people and every quote and number below are fictional and illustrative.

**Product:** Northpaid (fictional), payment-chasing software that sits on top of an existing invoicing tool.

## 0. Positioning inputs and assumptions

- Category: payment-reminder automation for small service businesses (from a product-positioning session).
- Best-fit customer: small creative agencies (5-30 people) with 10+ open invoices at any time.
- Primary alternative: the status quo, meaning the invoicing tool plus a spreadsheet plus manual emails.
- Assumed (flagged): the owner or an ops lead does the chasing; there is no dedicated finance person.

## 2. Six-part value proposition

| Part | Content | Evidence |
|------|---------|----------|
| Who | Owners and ops leads of creative agencies with 5-30 people and 10+ open invoices, with no finance team. Triggered by a cash-flow scare or a growing client list. **Not for:** freelancers (few invoices), agencies with an in-house finance team. | Q1, Q4, B1 |
| Why | "When invoices go overdue, I want them chased politely without my involvement, so I can keep cash flow predictable and keep good client relationships." | Q1, Q2, Q4 |
| What before | Invoicing tool + spreadsheet + manual emails each Friday; about 3 hours a week; awkward conversations; cash-flow surprises. | Q1, Q3, B1 |
| How | Reminder schedules per client in the owner's tone (presets and preview); stops automatically when payment is detected; overdue view; export in the accountant's format. | Features mapped to Q1, Q2, Q3, Q6 |
| What after | Chasing happens without the owner; who owes what is visible at a glance; fewer late payments. Target: days-to-paid down by 5+ (hypothesis H3). | Hypothesis |
| Alternatives | Status quo (above); basic reminders in the invoicing tool (robotic, no tone control); part-time bookkeeper (cost, availability); mid-market credit-control software (too heavy for 10 people). | Interviews, competitor-analysis |

| Anxiety | Q2, Q5 | 3 | Tone presets, preview, auto-stop on payment; message them near the call to action |

| **Net** | Push + Pull = 7, Anxiety + Habit = 6 | | Marginal: anxiety and habit decide the deal; the offer must address them |

## 4. Fit check

| Profile item (rank) | Reliever / creator | Score | Evidence |
|---------------------|--------------------|-------|----------|
| Pain 1: time spent chasing | Automated schedules | 2 | Concierge pilot: owners stopped manual chasing |
| Pain 2: being the bad cop | Tone presets + preview | 1 | Pilot comments only |
| Pain 3: cash-flow surprises | Overdue view | 1 | No forecasting yet |
| Gain 1: know who owes what | Overdue view | 2 | Pilot usage: weekly opens |
| Gain 2: get paid faster | Reminders | 1 | Hypothesis H3 |
| Gain 3: keep the accountant's process | — | 0 | Gap: export not built |

**Fit = 7 / 12 = 58% (partial).** Flags: Gain 3 has no reliever (build the export or narrow to agencies without an external accountant); custom invoice branding is an orphan feature for this segment (leave it out); Pain 2 relief is unevidenced (hypothesis H1).

## 5. Differentiation

| Alternative | Does well | Fails this segment | Switching cost | Our edge |
|-------------|-----------|--------------------|----------------|----------|
| Status quo | Free, full control of tone | Takes the owner's time; cash surprises | Habit, accountant's process | Automation in the owner's voice |
| Invoicing tool's reminders | Already installed | Robotic tone, no stop on bank-transfer payment | None | Tone control, auto-stop |
| Part-time bookkeeper | Human judgement | Cost, availability | Relationship | Always on, cheaper |
| Mid-market credit control | Powerful | Setup and price built for finance teams | Procedural | Ten-minute setup |

Swap test: "Northpaid chases overdue invoices in your own voice and stops the moment a client pays." The invoicing tool's reminders fail the swap (no tone, no auto-stop), so the difference holds, provided H1 passes.

## 7. Value statement and positioning statement

- **Value statement:** Small agencies get paid on time without spending Fridays chasing, because Northpaid sends reminders in the owner's own voice and stops the moment a client pays.
- **Positioning statement:** For small creative agencies who lose hours chasing late invoices, Northpaid is payment-reminder automation that keeps cash flowing without damaging client relationships. Unlike the reminders built into invoicing tools, it sounds like you and knows when a client has already paid.

## 8. Messaging hierarchy (handoff)

- **Headline:** Get paid on time without being the bad cop.
- **Subhead:** Northpaid chases overdue invoices for small agencies in your own voice, and stops the moment a client pays.
- **Pillar A, stop chasing:** proof: pilot owners stopped manual chasing (Q-pilot quotes); enabled by schedules and auto-stop.
- **Pillar B, keep clients happy:** proof pending H1 (claim not yet allowed in external copy beyond "preview every reminder"); enabled by tone presets and preview.
- **Pillar C, know who owes what:** proof: weekly use of the overdue view in the pilot; enabled by the overdue view and accountant export (export pending).
- **Objections:** "Will it annoy clients?" → preview and tone presets; "Will it break our accountant's process?" → export in her format (once built); "Another tool?" → works with your invoicing tool, ten-minute setup.
- **Phrase bank:** "chasing", "bad cop", "Friday afternoons", "who owes what". Avoid: "AR automation", "workflow optimisation".
- **Claims not yet allowed:** "paid 5 days faster" (H3), any price-value claim (H2).
